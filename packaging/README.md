# Cursed Apple Curiosities

Items, equipment and survivors adapted from Deadlock's item shop for Risk of Rain 2.

- **33 items** across white, green, red, void and lunar tiers, including **Lightning Scroll** (new in 1.8)
- **14 equipment**
- **Artifact of the Curio**: only this mod's items drop
- **Artifact of the Goose**: every player starts the run with a random Goose Egg
- **Curiosity Catalog** (lunar): clearing the teleporter drops a shop selling this mod's
  items, in exchange for opening chests
- **Cursed Broker**: a rare shrine (10% on any stage, 50% on stage 3 of each loop, and in the Bazaar Between
  Time). Curse one item to make every copy of it about twice as strong, in exchange for a harsh penalty for the
  rest of the run
- **Lash**, **Abrams** and **Seven** as playable survivors (more Deadlock heroes to come, one at a time), with Deadlock-style movement (stamina,
  air dashes, dash-jumps, slides, wall jumps)

## Expansion

All of the items, equipment, artifacts and the Cursed Broker form the **Cursed Apple Curiosities** expansion, which
you can switch on or off in the lobby next to the base game's expansions (on by default). Off, none of it drops or
appears; the survivors can still be played either way.

## Settings

The main numbers for every item, equipment and survivor can be changed in the Risk Of Options
mod settings page or in
`BepInEx/config/com.cursedapple.curiosities.cfg`.

## Multiplayer

Everyone in the lobby needs the mod, at the same version.

## Notes

If you own Deadlock, the survivors use their models, animations and voices from your
install (close Deadlock the first time you play). Otherwise they use a stand-in body.
